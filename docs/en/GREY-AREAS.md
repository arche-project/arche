# Grey areas — what French law allows for personal use

> Many self-reliance skills concern practices **regulated for sale or professional practice but free for oneself**. This page says where the line is, so the catalog stays legally solid and users know what they are doing. Not legal advice; check Légifrance / Service-Public. Full version in French: [ZONES-GRISES.md](../fr/ZONES-GRISES.md)

## The project's line, in three colours

| Colour | Definition | In the catalog? |
|---|---|---|
| **Green** | free for everyone | yes |
| **Grey** | regulated for sale, professional practice or scale, **but allowed for oneself** (or tolerated, or subject to a declaration) | **yes**, with the legal context in the entry and here |
| **Black** | forbidden even for oneself, or dangerous to the point that documenting it would harm: synthesis of medicines and antibiotics, controlled substances, weapons, explosives, security bypass | **no**, ever — editorial rule 7 of [COMMUNITY.md](COMMUNITY.md), non-negotiable, and a condition for institutional partnerships |

Knowledge itself is not illegal: reading a WHO monograph on a toxic plant or an army survival manual is legal. What the law frames is **selling, practising, transmitting, slaughtering, building, harvesting**. In a crisis laws do not vanish; the *state of necessity* (art. 122-7 of the French penal code) can justify an otherwise forbidden act against a present and imminent danger — proportionate and with no other way out. It is a defence after the fact, not a permit.

## Summary by domain (France)

- **Herbal medicine**: sale is a pharmacy monopoly except the **148 plants** of decree 2008-841; growing, drying, preparing teas, tinctures, salves **for oneself** is free; giving to others without therapeutic claims is tolerated; selling or advising "treatments" is not. Essential oils: steam distillation for oneself is free, but the **still** must be declared to customs. Medicines: a family pharmacy via your doctor's prescriptions is legal; online purchase without prescription or "aquarium antibiotics" are illegal in the EU and dangerous; making medicines is **black** in Arche. Narcotics: out of scope.
- **Food and farming**: seed exchange and sale to amateurs of non-catalogue varieties is legal since the 2020 law (art. L661-8 rural code); home brewing/wine/cider free, **distilling** alcohol without status is a customs offence; raw milk, cheese, preserves, charcuterie free at home (the risk is bacterial, not legal); poultry and rabbits may be slaughtered at home for the family, pigs/sheep/goats/cattle need an approved abattoir; hunting, fishing, trapping need permits; foraging tolerated for family use in public forests (prefectoral limits), protected species forbidden; wells need a town-hall declaration, rainwater is not for drinking while connected to the grid (different regime off-grid), dry toilets allowed (2009 order) with on-site composting.
- **Energy, housing, communication**: off-grid solar needs no utility paperwork but NF C 15-100 conditions insurance; grid-tied needs a declaration and Consuel certificate; self-building needs a permit or prior declaration and PLU compliance (the most common offence among off-gridders); listening to radio is free, **PMR446** and **LoRa/Meshtastic 868 MHz** are licence-free within power limits, amateur HF/VHF needs a licence, any means is allowed in a life-threatening emergency; drones over 250 g need registration; burning green waste is forbidden, outdoor rocket stoves are fine outside prefectoral fire bans.

## What it changes in Arche

Grey resources carry the `legal:grey` tag and link here; the beginner UI shows one sentence ("free for yourself, regulated for sale"). Health resources enter only with `reliability: reference` and two reviewers. The catalog references the **legal texts themselves** (Légifrance, Service-Public — Zimit recipe under decision) so users can check offline. Black is not discussed case by case: it does not enter, and a proposal that comes close is closed with a link to this page.
