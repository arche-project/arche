# 03 · Diarrhoea and dehydration — oral rehydration solution (ORS)

Diarrhoea kills through **dehydration**, especially children and the elderly. Treatment is simple and done at home.

## ORS recipe (WHO, home version)
- **1 litre of safe water** (sheet 01)
- **6 level teaspoons of sugar**
- **½ level teaspoon of salt**
Mix. Taste: no saltier than tears. Discard after 24 h.
No sugar: rice water (rice boiled long, lightly salted), or coconut water.

## How much
- After each loose stool: **child < 2: ¼–½ cup; child: ½–1 cup; adult: 1–2 cups**.
- Small frequent sips; by spoon for a vomiting child (wait 10 min after vomiting, resume).
- **Keep eating** (and breastfeeding). Zinc for children if available (10–20 mg/day, 10 days).

## Danger signs → seek help, keep giving ORS on the way
Sunken eyes, dry mouth, skin pinch stays up, little or no urine, very drowsy, blood in stool, high fever, diarrhoea > 3 days.

## Prevention
Wash hands **with soap** before eating and after the toilet; latrines at least 30 m from water and homes; cover food; treated water.
