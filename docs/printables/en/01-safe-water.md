# 01 · Making water safe to drink

**Rule**: any water that isn't verified tap water or sealed bottled water is suspect, even if clear.

## 1. Clarify (if cloudy)
Let stand 1 h, then filter through a clean cloth folded 4 times, or a sand/charcoal filter. Removes mud, **not germs**.

## 2. Disinfect — pick ONE method
| Method | How | Wait |
|---|---|---|
| **Boiling** (safest) | rolling boil for **1 min** (3 min above 2,000 m) | cool, covered |
| **Household bleach**, unscented, 2.6% (read the label) | **3 drops per litre** of clear water (6 if cloudy). Mix. | **30 min**. Faint chlorine smell = right. No smell = repeat. |
| **Tablets** (chlorine, dioxide) | dose on the leaflet | per leaflet (30 min to 4 h) |
| **Sun (SODIS)** | clear PET bottle ≤ 2 L, full, lying in the sun | **6 h** full sun, 2 days if cloudy |
| **Membrane filter** (0.1–0.2 µm) | per leaflet; doesn't remove viruses alone | add chlorine if in doubt |

## 3. Store
Clean, closed container, in the shade. Never dip hands or a dirty cup in it. Re-chlorinate after 24 h if in doubt.

## Do NOT
- drink roof rainwater untreated · trust "it looks clean" · use scented or "thick" bleach · exceed doses (taste = too much, not dangerous short-term).

**Needs**: 3 L/day/person to drink, 15 L/day with minimal hygiene (Sphere).
