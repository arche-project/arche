# 04 · Keeping food without a fridge

**Principle**: microbes need water, warmth, air and time. Remove at least one.

| Method | For | How | Keeps |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Drying** (sun, solar dryer, above the stove) | sliced fruit and vegetables, herbs, lean meat in thin salted strips | warm dry air, shade for herbs; done when it snaps (fruit: leathery) | months to 1 year, dry and sealed |
| **Salting** | meat, fish, pork fat | rub generously with coarse salt, stack, let water drain, re-salt; or 10% brine (100 g/L) | weeks to months, desalt before cooking |
| **Lacto-fermentation** | cabbage, carrots, turnips, cucumbers, beans | vegetables + **2% salt** (20 g/kg), packed under their juice, away from air, 20 °C, 1–3 weeks; then cool | months. Sour smell = normal; pink/black mould = discard |
| **Jar canning** | anything cooked, tomatoes, fruit, vegetables | clean jars, new seal, **100 °C in boiling water**: acidic fruit/tomatoes 30–45 min; **non-acidic vegetables/meat = botulism risk, 2–3 h or pressure canner** | 1 year. **Bulging lid, spurt on opening, off smell = discard without tasting** |
| **Jam / syrup** | fruit | 50–60% sugar, cook to the sheet test | 1 year |
| **Vinegar** | pickles, onions, blanched vegetables | vinegar ≥ 5%, covered | 6–12 months |
| **Fat / confit** | meat | cook in fat, cover entirely with fat | months, cool |
| **Cellar, clamp, sand** | potatoes, carrots, apples, cabbage | 4–10 °C, dark, ventilated; roots in damp sand | winter |
| **Eggs** | — | unwashed, point down, 15 °C: 3–4 weeks; lime water: months | |

**Grains and pulses**: dry (< 13% moisture, a grain cracks between teeth), sealed containers, bay leaves; watch for weevils.
