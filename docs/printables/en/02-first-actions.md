# 02 · Bleeding, wounds, burns — first actions

**First**: protect yourself (gloves, plastic bag), reassure, keep the person lying down if pale or dizzy.

## Heavy bleeding
1. **Press hard** directly on the wound with a clean cloth, without letting go, **10 min by the clock**. Add layers, don't remove the first.
2. If it soaks through and it's a limb: **tourniquet** 5–7 cm above the wound (belt, wide cloth + stick to twist), until bleeding stops. **Write the time** on the skin. Don't loosen.
3. Raise the limb. Cover the person (they will be cold).

## Simple wound
Wash with **plenty** of safe water (soap around, not inside), remove visible debris, cover with a clean dressing. Change daily. **Infection signs** (spreading redness, heat, pus, fever) → *Where There Is No Doctor*, "Wounds".

## Burn
**Cool with lukewarm water 15–20 min** (not ice-cold), remove rings/watch before swelling, cover with clean cling film or a lint-free cloth. **No** butter, toothpaste, ice. Don't pop blisters. Burn bigger than the palm, on face/hands/genitals, or deep (white, black, numb) = serious → protect, hydrate, seek help.

## Unconscious, breathing
On their side (recovery position), head tilted back, watch breathing.

## Not breathing
Compressions in the centre of the chest, **100–120/min, 5 cm deep**, without stopping. If trained: 30 compressions / 2 breaths.
