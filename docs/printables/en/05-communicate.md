# 05 · Communicating without a network

## Before the outage (do it now)
- Write on paper the **numbers and addresses** of relatives, a **meeting point** and a **plan B** ("if we can't reach each other, we meet at … on … at noon").
- A **battery or crank AM/FM radio**: public radio keeps broadcasting in a crisis (check your local frequencies).
- Optional: two **PMR446** walkie-talkies (licence-free in Europe, 446 MHz, 0.5 W, 1–5 km), rechargeable batteries + a small solar panel. Agree on a **channel** and listening times (e.g. 8:00, 12:00, 18:00 for 10 min).
- Further: **Meshtastic** (LoRa 868 MHz in Europe, text, several km, self-meshing) — ~€30 per module; or an amateur radio licence.

## During
- **Listen** before transmitting. Short messages: who, where, what, need. Repeat numbers.
- **Spell**: Alpha Bravo Charlie Delta Echo Foxtrot Golf Hotel India Juliett Kilo Lima Mike November Oscar Papa Quebec Romeo Sierra Tango Uniform Victor Whiskey X-ray Yankee Zulu.
- **Without radio**: notice board at the meeting point; message under an agreed stone; **whistle** (3 blasts = distress); **mirror** towards an aircraft/vehicle; ground signal **SOS** or **V** (need help) in 3 m branches/stones; 3 fires in a triangle = distress.
- **Night**: torch, 3 short flashes.

## Useful listening
- Weather: public radio bulletin. Without network: analogue barometer (fast drop = bad weather in 6–12 h) + the ESP32 weather sheet if installed.
- Amateur emergency frequencies (listening): 145.500 MHz (VHF), 7.090 / 3.690 MHz (HF, Europe). Transmitting without a licence is illegal **except in a life-threatening emergency**.
